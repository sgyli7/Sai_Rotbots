## Clearance and support budget, not a achieved geometry result

At the stationary tray/cover interface reserve 2 mm nominal static free gap; at moving forefoot/cover/lock passages begin with 5 mm motion-sweep allowance plus a separately declared deflection/tolerance budget. These are layout hypotheses, not release fits. A hidden mount tab can start at 3–4 mm thickness, 8–12 mm engagement with its own carrier and at least the same 2 mm clear gap from unrelated material. All tabs require real fastener/interface definition later.

The measured current selected fore tray is 0.1542736613 m² (54.02% of pad 0.2855891932 m²), because the existing rail material at Z=.033–.039 blocks parts of the slab. That is a current producer result, not an AA3 limit. Rebuild inner returns first and rerun exact material slab intersections. If pressure acts over unsupported edges, a real local lip/rib/pad compliance analysis is needed.
