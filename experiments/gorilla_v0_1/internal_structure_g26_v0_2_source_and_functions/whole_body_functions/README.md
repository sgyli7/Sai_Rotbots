# G26 V0.2 whole-body functional architecture input

This is a bounded, read-only whole-body deployment comparison. It selects no actuation architecture and creates no CAD. Latest V0.2 proportions take precedence over the older 2.65 m / 1.93 m targets. All whole-machine mass, COM, inertia, task performance and endurance remain unknown.

The actual b7 JSON/Blend and the independent extraction are bound by SHA in `input_bindings.json`. The 76 lower objects span **0.527734 × 0.377551 × 0.542300 normalized units**, with four appearance pitch locators per leg and three sole pieces on each shared foot frame. Blender METRIC / scale 1 does not establish SI. Use `p_SI = s R p_normalized + t` until whole-V0.2 registration provides `s`, orientation and the upper interface. The three known bad master meshes and their mirrored instances remain source defects; their visible volumes are not material or manufacturing evidence.

`function_budget.json` maps all **3072 historical G24 pieces once** to 16 function buckets, retaining exact IDs, source owner, role, literal mass range, aliases and unresolved owner scope. The four complete energy groups are identified by actual `g24_complete_group`, each with 262 pieces; cells and auxiliaries are not split by guessed name prefixes. Existing 18 holding and 18 directional groups are traced individually as historical function inventory, not a required V0.2 actuator or valve count. The first table and its exact producer are retained under `first_table_snapshot/`; only the final bucket metadata was corrected, without changing source geometry or the architecture arithmetic.

Both candidates retain wide closed load paths from shoulders and waist through pelvis and feet; complete drive input/output/support/brake/control interfaces; both hands' opposition, grasp/release and tool manipulation; three actual wrist orientation freedoms; full HV/LV safety; active liquid and air heat rejection; autonomy sensors/compute and all fixed/flexible interconnects. Five hand drives are an existing research direction, not a user-frozen hardware requirement. Arm, waist and leg counts in the comparison are hypotheses; no stable ABI is claimed.

The comparison includes the extra connection, installation and cooling cost of shorter distributed packs. Cell mass is **unknown at low/nominal and at most 50.4 kg for all 720 from the source 70 g maximum**. Auxiliary mass is never scaled away with cell count. Raw 72s voltage, cell temperature/current, DC-link/precharge, isolation, local protection, regen or dump, critical and auxiliary rails must be reconciled with the selected complete drives; old 80s behavior and old task energy do not qualify the new body.

`conservation_model` records the executable accounting boundaries: disjoint material plus actual OEM components plus fluid once; actual descendant 6D wrench and output/support separation; motor/gear at a common speed, torque, bus and temperature; cylinder cap/annulus forces and full length Jacobian; power and negative-work losses; coolant/oil lumen, device chamber and working fill inventory; and nonzero wire/hose/connector/support mass. Broad appearance space and outer bounding boxes do not prove an installable cavity.

The next complete comparison needs whole-source SI registration, load/contact and manipulation domains, actual owner trees and support interfaces, and complete source-bound installation/thermal/service envelopes. Historical missing owners, mixed bearing/rotor bodies, possible duplicated central waist references, the missing right-side gear accounting and the old lower placeholder stay open. Qualified retirement mass credit is **zero**. No unchanged piece, successful ID check or bare component reference constitutes structural, sustained-drive, heat, electrical or autonomy release.

Reproduce the read-only table with `.venv/bin/python .scratch/gorilla_internal_g26_whole_body_function_budget/build_budget.py`; this overwrites only this branch's generated JSON, so preserve the frozen outputs before rerunning. `verify_budget.py` independently verifies source identity, once-only membership, complete energy groups and integer cell/group/interface arithmetic without importing the producer. No source publication files were copied or edited.
