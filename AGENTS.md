# Project instructions

Before creating, moving or renaming files, read and follow
[sai_robots_engineering_rules.md](sai_robots_engineering_rules.md), the single
source of directory, naming, artifact and migration rules for this repository.

Before presenting Goose product design, CAD views, or appearance work as finished,
read and apply [the delivery design-review gate](robots/goose_v0_2/design/delivery_review_gate.json).
It links the required industrial-design and Steve Jobs review skills. Correct
failed design checks at their source and repeat the affected views before claiming
completion; label interim concepts and unevaluated engineering scope explicitly.
